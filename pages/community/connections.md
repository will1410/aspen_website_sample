## Community Groups

Get involved by joining one of the groups driven by Aspen community members. Anyone using Aspen or interested in learning more about Aspen is welcome to attend.

Scroll down to the Community Calendar below to copy any meeting to your own calendar and/or view Zoom link information.

All agendas, notes, recordings can be found in the Aspen Community Shared Partner Folder and in relevant sections below.

### Aspen Community Gathering

What: Discuss questions, topics of interest, and future developments with other libraries using Aspen.

Aspen Community Meeting Agendas and Notes

Please note the Aspen Community Meeting and Aspen Gathering have merged into Aspen Community Gathering. Community Gatherings will be held monthly, every second Thursday (starting April 10th, 2025) at 8am PST / 9 am MST /10 am CST / 11 am EST

### Aspen Technical Committee

What: The Technical Committee reviews code contributions, manages project releases, manages rights within the project’s software repository, and participates in strategic planning.

When: Monthly on Third Tuesdays, 8am PST / 9 am MST /10 am CST / 11 am EST

### Aspen Automated Testing

What: Community members work together to create and refine automated tests for Aspen. This group uses the #testing slack channel.

When: Bi-monthly on First and Fourth Tuesdays, 11:00 am Eastern / 8:00 am Pacific

Aspen Automaged Testing Meeting Agendas and notes

### Aspen Catalogers Group

What: Catalogers unite! Come to discuss cataloging-related topics and questions related to Aspen. This group uses the #catalogers Slack channel.

When: Monthly on Third Thursdays, 2:00-3:30 pm Eastern / 11:00-12:30 am Pacific

Agendas and Notes

### Aspen for Consortiums

What: Does your consortium use Aspen? Are you interested in discussing how Aspen can continue to support consortia of all types and sizes? Join us! This group uses the #consortium Slack channel.

When: Bi-monthly on Third Wednesdays, 2:00 pm Eastern / 11:00 am Pacific

Agendas and Notes

### Aspen Developers Group

What: Interested in developing for Aspen? Come to discuss development-related topics and questions related to Aspen. This group uses the #developers Slack channel.

When: Monthly on Second Tuesdays, 11:00 am Eastern / 8:00 am Pacific

Learn more about developing for Aspen at https://dev.aspendiscovery.org/

Agendas and Notes

### Aspen Community Issue Triage

What: This group looks at questions and issues submitted to the community Jira and puts our heads together to come up with answers and/or next steps. No experience is needed to join us, and it's a great place to learn more about how Aspen works! This group uses the #jira-review Slack channel.

When: First and Third Monday of the month at 11:00 am Eastern / 8:00 am Pacific

Agendas and Notes

### Aspen Documentation Group

What: This group is dedicated to the centralization and organization of available Aspen documentation.

When: Monthly on the fourth Thursday at 1:00pm Eastern / 10:00am pacific.

Agendas and Notes

### LiDA Users Group

What: Interested in connecting with other LiDA users? Come to discuss any and all things LiDA! This group uses the #app Slack channel.

When: Every two months on the fourth Wednesday, 11:00 am Eastern / 8:00 am Pacific

Agendas and Notes

### ILS Groups

Get together with other libraries using Aspen and your ILS. Don't see a group for your ILS here? Let us know if you'd like to start a user group!

- Bi-monthly on Third Tuesdays, 3:00 pm Eastern / 12:00 pm Pacific
- Agendas and Notes
- #symphony Slack channel

#### Aspen Polaris User Group

- Agendas and Notes
- #polaris Slack channel

#### Aspen for Evergreen Users
- Bi-monthly on First Thursdays, 12:00 pm Eastern / 9:00 am Pacific (even months)
- Agendas and Notes
- #evergreen Slack channel

#### Aspen for CarlX Users
- Monthly on First Wednesdays, 2:00 pm Eastern / 11:00 am Pacific
- Conversation Topics

#### Aspen for Sierra Users

- Agendas and Notes
- #koha Slack channel

### Working Groups

Working groups are formed for a limited time with the goal of achieving a specific task or goal. There are no active working groups at this time.
